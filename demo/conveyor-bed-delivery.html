What the station actually calls

  1. GET /conveyor/next-job → 204 No Content
  2. POST /prescriptions → 201 Created
  3. GET /conveyor/next-job → 200 {delivery_id, bed_no:"2"}
  4. POST /deliveries/{id}/dispatch → 200 in_transit
  5. POST /deliveries/{id}/mark-arrived → 200 arrived
  6. GET /conveyor/next-job → 204 No Content

Two human actions in the whole run: the doctor writes the prescription, and someone presses one button on the machine. Everything between is the station talking to the backend by itself. This build has no scanning step — it automates and records delivery, it does not verify it.
